📈 What are tokenized stocks?

Tokenized stocks like xStocks are crypto tokens that track the price of real equities (AAPL, TSLA, NVDA…). They live on blockchains like Solana, so they can be held in a crypto wallet and traded 24/7 — no brokerage hours, no settlement delays.

⚡ Why Solana?

Solana settles transactions in ~400 milliseconds for a fraction of a cent. That makes it practical to trade stock exposure on-chain the way you'd tap through a brokerage app — fast quotes, instant fills, always open.

🧪 What is paper trading?

Paper trading means practicing with virtual money against real market prices. You learn order types, position sizing, and how P&L works — without risking a dollar. Every price on xPaper comes from live Solana DEX markets; every dollar in your account is simulated.
